description: "How VolksAI combines deterministic spatial parsing with Claude LLM fallback for maximum accuracy and minimal token cost"

Rather than immediately feeding hundreds of PDF pages to an expensive Large Language Model, VolksAI employs a **Two-Layer Extraction Architecture**.

## Architectural Comparison

```mermaid
graph TD
    PDF[Tender PDF Package] --> PyMuPDF[PyMuPDF / pdfplumber Text & Layout Engine]
    
    subgraph Layer 1: Deterministic Engine (0 Tokens)
        PyMuPDF --> L1Rules[Spatial Table & Regex Parser]
        L1Rules -->|Standard Bid Data Found| L1Match[High Confidence Fields]
    end
    
    subgraph Layer 2: LLM Fallback (Anthropic Claude)
        L1Rules -->|Missing Fields| Role1[Role 1: Claude Haiku 4.5]
        L1Rules -->|Ambiguous / Conflicting Clauses| Role2[Role 2: Claude Sonnet 5.5]
        
        Role1 -->|Structured Tool Call| AIResolved[AI Resolved Fields]
        Role2 -->|Legal Reasoning & Resolution| AIResolved
    end
    
    L1Match --> DTO[Normalized TMS DTO]
    AIResolved --> DTO
    L1Rules -->|Clause Not in Document| Missing[Missing Fields Array]
    Missing --> DTO
```

---

## Layer 1: Deterministic Rules & Spatial Layout

**File:** `volksAi/app/services/tender_mapper.py` & `field_extractor.py`

Layer 1 operates entirely without external API calls. It uses spatial bounding boxes and deterministic regular expressions tuned for standard Indian Government procurement formats:

* **GeM Standard Header Tables:** Captures Bid Number (`GEM/2026/B/...`), Bid End Date & Time, Organization Name, Ministry, Department, and Total Quantity.
* **Earnest Money Deposit (EMD):** Extracts required amount, advisory bank, and exemption eligibility.
* **Performance Bank Guarantee (PBG):** Extracts required percentage and validity months from structured Bid Data Sheets.
* **Basic Bidder Criteria:** Identifies turnover requirements and past experience years when displayed in standard two-column tables.

<Tip>
  **Performance Benefit:** Layer 1 executes in **under 1.5 seconds** and incurs **0 token cost**. Over 65% of standardized GeM fields are resolved entirely within Layer 1.
</Tip>

---

## Layer 2: Scoped LLM Fallback (Anthropic Claude)

**File:** `volksAi/app/services/llm_field_resolver.py`

When Layer 1 fails to find a critical parameter or detects conflicting clauses across multiple document pages, Layer 2 is conditionally invoked:

1. **Missing Fields:** If a field is `None` after Layer 1 (for example, special payment milestones buried in an ATC narrative), **Role 1 (Claude Haiku 4.5)** is triggered with scoped clause snippets.
2. **Ambiguity Resolution:** If Layer 1 detects multiple conflicting values (e.g. general terms state 90-day delivery, but Special Conditions state 120-day delivery), **Role 2 (Claude Sonnet 5.5)** evaluates the contract hierarchy and provides legal reasoning.

## Confidence Scoring & Badge Model

Every field emitted by the VolksAI pipeline is tagged with an audit trail and confidence level reflected directly in the React frontend:

| Badge | Confidence Level | Source | Meaning to Tender Executive |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| <span className="text-emerald-500 font-bold">GREEN</span> | **High Confidence** | Layer 1 Deterministic | Extracted directly from a standardized table or rigid regex pattern. Highly reliable. |
| <span className="text-amber-500 font-bold">AMBER</span> | **Review Needed / AI Resolved** | Layer 2 Claude LLM | Resolved by Claude from unstructured clauses or reconciled after conflicting statements. Hovering displays the model's exact legal reasoning and source quote. |
| <span className="text-red-500 font-bold">RED</span> | **Missing / Not Found** | None | The clause does not exist anywhere in the uploaded document. Requires manual data entry by the engineer. |

<Info>
  **Audit Trail Guarantee:** For every Amber field, VolksAI saves `{field_name}_reasoning` and `{field_name}_clause_excerpt` into PostgreSQL, ensuring human bidding engineers can verify AI assertions in one click.
</Info>
