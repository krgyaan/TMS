The following terms appear frequently in the codebase, database schemas, and government tender documents:

## Public Procurement & Bidding Terms

| Term | Full Name | Plain-Language Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **GeM** | Government e-Marketplace | The Indian Government's centralized procurement portal (`gem.gov.in`) where central ministries, state bodies, and PSUs acquire goods and services. |
| **ATC** | Additional Terms & Conditions | A supplementary PDF appended to a standardized GeM bid by the buyer containing custom clauses that often override general terms. |
| **BEC / PQR** | Bidder Eligibility Criteria / Pre-Qualification Requirements | Mandatory qualifying hurdles (e.g. minimum annual turnover, 7 years in business, 3 completed contracts of ₹1 Cr). Failing any single criterion disqualifies the bidder immediately. |
| **GCC** | General Conditions of Contract | Standard, non-negotiable contract clauses common across all government purchases. |
| **SCC** | Special Conditions of Contract | Contract clauses tailored specifically to an individual project. Under Indian contract law, **SCC clauses legally supersede GCC clauses**. |
| **SOW** | Scope of Work | Detailed specification of engineering deliverables, installations, equipment supply, and technical parameters. |
| **BDS** | Bid Data Sheet | A summary table in public tenders listing due dates, bid submission address, EMD amounts, and nodal officer contacts. |
| **IFB / NIT** | Invitation for Bids / Notice Inviting Tender | The formal public advertisement announcing that a tender has been issued. |
| **EMD** | Earnest Money Deposit | A financial deposit (typically 1% to 2% of tender value via Bank Guarantee or Demand Draft) paid at submission to prevent frivolous bids. Fully refunded to unsuccessful bidders. |
| **ePBG / PBG** | (Electronic) Performance Bank Guarantee | A bank guarantee (typically 3% to 10% of order value) submitted by the winning contractor to guarantee contract completion. Forfeited if the contractor fails to deliver. |
| **CPS / SD** | Contract Performance Security / Security Deposit | Synonymous with PBG in many PSU contracts (e.g., GAIL, NTPC), representing financial security held during the contract and warranty period. |
| **PRS / LD** | Price Reduction Schedule / Liquidated Damages | A contractual penalty deducted from contractor invoices if delivery is delayed (typically 0.5% per week of delay up to a maximum cap of 5% to 10%). |
| **MSE / MSME** | Micro and Small Enterprises | Government of India certified small businesses. Under Indian public procurement policy, MSEs are frequently granted **100% exemption from paying EMD and tender fees**. |
| **MII** | Make in India Policy | Policy granting price preference to bidders whose equipment contains a minimum percentage of local Indian manufacturing value addition (Class-I / Class-II local suppliers). |
| **OEM** | Original Equipment Manufacturer | The original manufacturer of equipment (e.g., ABB, Schneider, Siemens) as opposed to a distributor or EPC system integrator. |
| **MAF** | Manufacturer's Authorization Form | A formal letter on OEM letterhead authorizing a system integrator (like Volks Energie) to supply and service the OEM's products for a specific tender. |
| **L1** | Lowest Evaluated Bidder | In government bidding, contracts are awarded to the bidder who passes all technical criteria and submits the lowest financial price (the "L1" bidder). |
| **LOA / FOA** | Letter of Award / Fax of Acceptance | The official award letter issued by the buyer notifying the contractor that they have won the tender, legally starting the project delivery clock. |
| **CRAC** | Consignee Receipt and Acceptance Certificate | A digital certificate generated on GeM by the buyer confirming receipt and quality approval of delivered goods, mandatory before invoice payment is released. |
| **SITC** | Supply, Installation, Testing & Commissioning | A turnkey contract scope where the contractor is responsible for the full lifecycle: buying material, installing it on site, and commissioning it. |
