## What is TMS & VolksAI?

The **Tender Management System (TMS)** is Volks Energie's enterprise operations platform managing tendering workflows, HRMS, operations, purchase orders, work orders, CRM, finance, and business analytics.

Paired with **VolksAI**—a high-performance microservice combining spatial PDF parsing with Claude LLM fallback—TMS automates the painful, error-prone manual analysis of dense government tender packages.

## The Real-World Problem We Solve

Every morning, Tender Executives at Volks Energie review Indian Government and Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) tenders—such as **GAIL, IOCL, NTPC, Indian Railways, and State Electricity Boards**—ranging from 50 to 400+ pages of dense legal clauses.

Historically, bidding engineers had to manually comb through hundreds of pages under tight submission deadlines to locate critical parameters:

* **Earnest Money Deposit (EMD):** Financial upfront deposit required to bid
* **Performance Bank Guarantee (PBG / CPS):** Security deposit required upon contract award
* **Liquidated Damages / Price Reduction (LD / PRS):** Delay penalty rates per week and liability caps
* **Payment Terms & Milestones:** Exact percentages for supply vs. installation and commissioning
* **Bidder Eligibility Criteria (BEC / PQR):** Mandatory turnover, past similar contract values, and joint venture rules

<Info>
  **The Cost of a Human Error:** Missing a single clause buried on page 183 of an Additional Terms & Conditions (ATC) PDF could result in forfeiting a ₹50,00,000 bank guarantee or immediate disqualification.
</Info>
